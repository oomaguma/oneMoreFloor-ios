# Privacy Policy — One More Floor

**Last updated: May 27, 2026**

This privacy policy describes how One More Floor ("the app", "we", "us") handles your information.

---

## Data We Collect

One More Floor does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information to our own servers. All game progress (floors reached, gold, upgrades, settings) is saved locally on your device using Apple's standard storage and is never sent anywhere.

---

## Advertising

The app displays ads powered by **Google AdMob**. If you consent to ad personalization, AdMob may collect your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) to show you relevant ads and measure their effectiveness. This data is processed by Google under their own privacy policy.

You will be shown an App Tracking Transparency prompt before any identifier is accessed. You can change your choice at any time in **Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking**.

Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

---

## In-App Purchases

Purchases (Remove Ads, Night Watch) are processed entirely by Apple. We do not receive or store your payment information. See Apple's privacy policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

---

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

---

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new "Last updated" date.
