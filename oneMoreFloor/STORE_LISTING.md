# One More Floor — App Store Listing (draft)

Draft ASO copy for the overhaul relaunch. These are first-principles drafts — refine
the keyword field against a real ASO tool / competitor scan before submitting. App
Store character limits are noted in parentheses.

The positioning hook the whole listing leans on: **"How greedy do you dare to be?"** —
push-your-luck is the differentiator, so it leads.

## App name (≤30 chars)
Adding a genre keyword to the name is worth real ranking, so:
- **Recommended:** `One More Floor: Idle RPG` (24)
- Brand only: `One More Floor` (14)
- Alt: `One More Floor: Dungeon RPG` (27)

## Subtitle (≤30 chars)
Prime keyword real estate — don't waste it on fluff.
- **Recommended:** `Push-your-luck dungeon RPG` (26)
- Alts: `Roguelite idle dungeon dive` (27) · `Tap, climb, bank, repeat` (24)

## Description

> ⚔️ ONE MORE FLOOR — just one more...
>
> A dungeon-climbing RPG built on one irresistible question: **how greedy do you dare to be?**
>
> Tap to fight your way down. Every floor you clear without resting cranks your GREED — fatter gold, better loot. But everything you earn is AT RISK until you bank it at a camp. Die, and it's gone. Push too far and lose it all… or play it safe and miss the big haul.
>
> Just. One. More. Floor.
>
> ⚔️ ACTIVE COMBAT — Tap to strike, dodge telegraphed heavy attacks, and unleash each hero's signature ability. Or flip on Auto and let it ride.
> 🔥 PUSH YOUR LUCK — The deeper you go, the richer the rewards — and the more you stand to lose.
> 🛡️ EIGHT HEROES — From the glass-cannon Wizard to the unkillable Knight Templar, each plays differently. Retire heroes to build a dynasty across runs.
> 👹 BOSS FIGHTS — Bosses enrage at low health, raise shields, and heal. Read them and time your strikes.
> 💎 DEEP LOOT — Five rarities, set bonuses, and gear that scales as you descend.
> 🌙 OFFLINE PROGRESS — Your hero keeps farming while you're away.
> ♾️ PRESTIGE — Reset for permanent power and climb even higher.
>
> Free to play. How deep will you get?
